# Longitudinal Analysis of Poverty in Spain (2008–2021)

Master's Thesis completed in the Master's Degree in Economic Research (UNED).

Languaje: Spanish.

## Objective

Analyse poverty incidence, intensity, persistence and mobility in Spain using longitudinal microdata from the Survey of Living Conditions (ECV/EU-SILC).

## Main findings

- Poverty remained around 20%.
- Covid-19 increased poverty intensity.
- Probability of exiting poverty was approximately 36%. That probability has decreased in recent years.
- Initial income level strongly predicts poverty persistence.
- Employment quality is closely associated with poverty risk.
- Determinating charactesistics: number of minors and age or educational level of the head of household is a determining factor in the risk of poverty.

## Data

Source:

- Living Conditions Survey (ECV)
- Spanish National Statistics Institute (INE)

## Methodology

- Construction of a longitudinal household panel.
- Poverty incidence measurement (at-risk-of-poverty rate).
- Poverty gap analysis.
- Persistent poverty analysis.
- Poverty mobility analysis.
- Chi-square association testing.
